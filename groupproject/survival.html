Survival Systems

Master the core mechanics that define life and death.

Rain Cycle

The rain cycle is the most important survival mechanic. Each cycle begins with a limited amount of time before catastrophic rainfall wipes out all life in the region.

How It Works

  • A hidden timer begins when you leave a shelter
  • Environmental cues signal when rain is approaching
  • Rain becomes instantly lethal once it begins

Player Strategy

  • Memorize shelter locations in each region
  • Never explore too far without a return plan
  • Use shortcuts and tunnels to move efficiently

Why It Matters

The rain cycle creates constant pressure, forcing players to balance exploration with survival. It defines the pacing of the entire game.

Food & Hibernation

To survive each cycle, the player must consume enough food to hibernate safely in a shelter.

How It Works

  • Food pips must be filled before hibernation
  • Different creatures provide different food values
  • Failure to eat enough prevents saving progress

Player Strategy

  • Prioritize easy prey like batflies
  • Avoid risky hunts unless necessary
  • Store extra food when possible

Why It Matters

This system ties survival directly to exploration and risk-taking, forcing players to engage with the ecosystem.

Karma System

Karma represents your progression and ability to access new areas of the world.

How It Works

  • Successfully hibernating increases karma
  • Death lowers karma
  • Certain gates require specific karma levels

Player Strategy

  • Play cautiously to avoid losing progress
  • Farm safer areas to rebuild karma
  • Learn enemy patterns to reduce deaths

Why It Matters

Karma acts as both a reward and punishment system, encouraging careful play and mastery of mechanics.

Movement & Traversal

Movement is physics-based and requires precision, timing, and awareness of the environment.

Key Mechanics

  • Climbing poles and walls
  • Swimming through water systems
  • Using momentum for jumps

Player Strategy

  • Practice movement in safe areas
  • Use terrain to escape predators
  • Stay above danger when possible

Why It Matters

Mastery of movement is essential for survival, as it determines whether you can escape threats and navigate complex regions.

Predator Awareness

The ecosystem is alive, and creatures behave dynamically based on sight, sound, and movement.

How It Works

  • Predators track movement and sound
  • Some rely on sight, others on vibration
  • Behavior varies by species

Player Strategy

  • Move slowly to avoid detection
  • Use distractions like thrown objects
  • Learn each predator’s behavior

Why It Matters

Understanding predator behavior turns the game from random survival into strategic decision-making.
