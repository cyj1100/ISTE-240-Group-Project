Creatures

Learn predator and prey behavior.

Lizard

Type: Predator

Threat: High

Tracks movement visually and aggressively chases prey.

Vulture

Type: Predator

Threat: Very High

Aerial predator that swoops down quickly.

Batfly

Type: Prey

Threat: None

A primary food source.

Salamander

Type: Predator (Aquatic)

Threat: High

Fast underwater hunters that drag prey below.

Pole Plant

Type: Ambush Predator

Threat: Medium

Disguised as climbable poles. When grabbed, they lash out and pull prey upward. Always test poles with thrown objects first.

Monster Kelp

Type: Aquatic Predator

Threat: High

Lurks in water and grabs anything that swims too close. Struggles are rarely successful—avoid entirely when possible.

Leeches

Type: Swarm Predator

Threat: Medium

Swarm in water and latch onto the player, slowing movement and pulling them down. Staying on land or moving quickly is key to survival.

Centipedes

Type: Predator

Threat: Medium

Electrified creatures that stun prey on contact. Larger variants are more dangerous and harder to escape.

Centiwings

Type: Predator

Threat: Medium

A flying variant of centipedes that can chase prey through the air, making them harder to avoid than ground-based versions.

Coalescipede

Type: Swarm Predator

Threat: High

Small spider-like creatures that merge into a larger mass when grouped, becoming significantly more dangerous together.

Spiders

Type: Swarm Predator

Threat: High

Hunt in groups and overwhelm prey quickly. Light sources can help deter them in darker regions.

Dropwig

Type: Ambush Predator

Threat: High

Hides on ceilings and drops onto prey below. Look for subtle movement before passing underneath.

Leviathan

Type: Apex Predator

Threat: Extreme

Massive aquatic predators that dominate deep waters. Escape is nearly impossible if one targets you.

Miros Bird

Type: Predator

Threat: High

Fast, aggressive creatures that patrol specific areas. Their speed makes outrunning them very difficult.

Brother Long Legs

Type: Blind Predator

Threat: Very High

Tracks prey through sound rather than sight. Slow, careful movement is essential to avoid detection.

Daddy Long Legs

Type: Apex Predator

Threat: Extreme

Highly dangerous and nearly impossible to escape once grabbed. Avoid entirely and stay silent in their presence.

Worm Grass

Type: Environmental Hazard

Threat: High

Appears as normal grass but pulls prey underground when stepped on. Avoid walking through dense patches.

Overseer

Type: Observer / Guide

Threat: None

Floating, mechanical-like entities that observe the world. Some guide the player by projecting symbols or images. They are not hostile but may influence exploration.
